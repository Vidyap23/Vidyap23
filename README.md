## 🚀 Key Experience & Projects

### 🔊 Real-Time Systems & AI Infrastructure
I work on real-time, low-latency systems that sit at the intersection of networking and AI. My recent work includes building QUIC-based audio streaming systems, integrating DSP and turn-taking logic, and productionizing streaming text-to-speech and inference pipelines across multiple cloud providers.

### ⚙️ Distributed Systems & Backend Engineering
I enjoy designing backend systems from first principles—event-driven architectures, schedulers, and internal frameworks that improve system responsiveness and reliability. I’ve worked on notification systems, workflow integrations, and scalable analytics platforms, often in fast-moving product environments.

### 🔬 Research & Systems Programming
My background includes systems-level research and tooling, from building containerized analysis environments to writing custom plugins and implementing distributed protocols and schedulers. I’m comfortable working close to the OS, network, and runtime when needed.
